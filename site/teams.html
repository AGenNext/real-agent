Multi-team

One platform. Many teams. Each governs its own agents.

Onboard a department without entangling it with the rest of the organization — isolated, yet able to collaborate.

Tenancy

Isolation by default.

Each team operates as a tenant. Authority, policy, data, audit, and trust are scoped to the team — a misconfigured agent in one team can't reach another's resources.

  • Namespace-level isolation per team
  • Independent authority & policy boundaries
  • Separate audit log and trust per team
  • No implicit cross-team access

Identity

Portable identity, nomadic by design.

A single identity can carry across teams without copying it into each one — inspired by nomadic-identity systems. You stay you; your authority is granted, per team, where it's needed.

Federation

A network between agents.

Agents don't operate in isolation. The platform models the relationships between them — delegation, hand-off, and shared context — so an agent network can form across teams while governance stays intact.

Delegate

An agent hands a sub-task to another, carrying authority and audit with it.

Relate

Typed relationships connect agents, tools, decisions, and outcomes as one graph.

Govern

Every cross-agent interaction is still policy-checked and logged.
